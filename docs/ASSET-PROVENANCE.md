# Asset Provenance

All figures referenced by the revised learning material are local SVG teaching diagrams created for this AI-assisted revision on 26 October 2026. They are original explanatory drawings, not screenshots, packet captures, benchmarks or experimental evidence. Their technical concepts are supported by the sources in the corresponding chapters. No blanket license over earlier contributors’ work is asserted.

The 35 remote Hardware image insertions and 20 unused raster files from the baseline were superseded. Their earlier versions remain recoverable in Git history. New drawings correct ambiguous register/cache ordering, PC/IR timing, LOAD address generation, compiler scope and the placement of NIC hardware.
